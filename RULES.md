# Rules & Assumptions

| What | Value | Why | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Salaried FOIR | 50% | Industry standard for personal loans (RBI/NHB) | My judgement |
| Self-employed FOIR | 40% | Higher income volatility | My judgement |
| Informal FOIR | 30% | Unstable cash flows | My judgement |
| Safe surplus utilisation | 60% | Leaves 40% for emergencies & savings | My judgement |
| Base living cost | ₹15,000 | Minimum monthly survival spend (single) | My judgement |
| Extra per dependent | ₹5,000 | Additional household cost | My judgement |
| Salaried rate band | 10.5% – 12.5% | Top PSU/private bank rates (SBI/HDFC) | My judgement |
| Secured LAP rate | 8.5% – 10.5% | Lower risk due to collateral | My judgement |
| Unsecured self-employed rate | 14% – 18% | Higher risk than salaried | My judgement |
| Informal/gig rate | 24% – 32% | NBFC / microfinance pricing | My judgement |
| Processing fee (salaried) | 2.0% | Amortised into APR | My judgement |
| Processing fee (secured LAP) | 1.0% | Lower because of collateral | My judgement |
| Processing fee (informal) | 3.0% | Higher origination cost | My judgement |
| Tenure (salaried) | 5 years | Standard personal loan | My judgement |
| Tenure (secured LAP) | 15 years | Longer-term secured product | My judgement |
| Tenure (informal) | 3 years | Micro-loans are shorter | My judgement |
| Default credit score (salaried) | 680 | Assumed average if unknown | My judgement |
| Default credit score (self-employed) | 600 | Assumed lower due to informal income | My judgement |
| Default credit score (informal) | 500 | No formal credit history | My judgement |
| "Don't borrow" trigger | Existing EMI / Income > 60% OR recent bounce | Prevents debt trap | My judgement |
| Unknown credit score effect | Widen rate band by ±1.5% | Reflects lender uncertainty | My judgement |
